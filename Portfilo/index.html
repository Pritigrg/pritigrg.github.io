Projects

Research · 2025

Body-Part Aware Prompting for Person Re-Identification

Explores how body-part-aware prompting can improve reliability of vision-language models for person re-identification by guiding model attention toward semantically meaningful body regions.

  • Proposed a body-part-aware prompting strategy to improve attention localization and identity matching robustness
  • Accepted to the AWS Machine Learning University AI/ML Student Poster Session 2025
  • Reflects broader interest in interpretable multimodal systems aligned with task structure
Research · 2025

Extended TVL Dataset for Material Classification

Extends the Touch-Vision-Language (TVL) dataset with material-type annotations to support multimodal material understanding, combining dataset development, tactile representation learning, and label quality analysis.

  • Extended dataset with material-level labels and built a tactile-only encoder-based classifier
  • Investigated agreement between LLM-annotated and human-labeled categories
  • Studied whether tactile signals alone can classify visually ambiguous materials
Research · 2025

LOOM — Feature Refinement for Person Re-Identification

A lightweight, training-free feature refinement method based on leave-one-out mean centering that improves embedding quality at inference time without retraining the base model.

  • Proposed identity-aware and cluster-aware variants for supervised and label-free settings
  • Improved feature separability while reducing intra-class variance
  • Efficient enough for inference-time use with no architecture changes
Research · 2024

Risk Profiling with LLMs and Few-Shot Prompting

Uses large language models and few-shot prompting to automate risk assessment from social media data, producing interpretable scores across multiple decision dimensions.

  • Designed a 0–10 scoring framework for financial stability, cybersecurity, and reputation management
  • Built the pipeline to support analysis at social-media scale
  • Presented as an accepted poster at NeurIPS Women in Machine Learning Workshop
Research · 2024

Trustworthy Human-Autonomy Teaming

Investigates neuro-symbolic reinforcement learning for trustworthy human-autonomy teaming, connecting learning-based control with structured reasoning for safer and more interpretable AI behavior.

  • Explored neuro-symbolic RL framed around assurance and security for AI-enabled systems
  • Oral presentation at SPIE Assurance and Security for AI-enabled Systems, 2024
