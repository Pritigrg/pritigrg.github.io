Priti Gurung

Ph.D. Candidate in Computer Science at Howard University

I am a third-year Computer Science Ph.D. student at Howard University, advised by Dr. Jiang Li and Dr. Danda B. Rawat. My research sits at the intersection of computer vision, multimodal AI, and physical world understanding.

Before my Ph.D., I earned a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Information Technology from Tribhuvan University, Nepal, and spent four years in industry.
