A series of applied AI projects completed as part of the CodePath AI 110 Fellowship program. Each module focuses on building practical AI systems with real-world datasets and problem settings.

**Modules completed:**

- **Game Glitch Investigator** — AI-powered debugging and anomaly detection for game systems
- **PawPal** — Intelligent pet recommendation and matching system
- **The Mood Machine** — Sentiment and emotion classification using natural language processing

All implementations are in Python, with a focus on building end-to-end pipelines from data preprocessing through model evaluation.
