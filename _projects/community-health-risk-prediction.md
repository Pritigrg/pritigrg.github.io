Chronic diseases such as diabetes, hypertension, and asthma are not distributed evenly across communities. Health outcomes are strongly influenced by social determinants of health — income, education, employment, and housing — as well as environmental exposures such as air pollution, extreme heat, and climate-related stressors.

Most existing analyses treat these factors in isolation. This project addresses that gap by developing a multimodal machine learning system that integrates demographic, socioeconomic, and environmental data to predict chronic disease risk at the county level across the United States.

**Key contributions:**

- Unified predictive framework combining demographic, socioeconomic, and environmental modalities
- County-level risk prediction for chronic diseases including diabetes, hypertension, and asthma
- Designed to support public health agencies in identifying high-risk communities for targeted intervention
