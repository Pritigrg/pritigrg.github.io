This project extends the Touch-Vision-Language (TVL) dataset with material-type annotations to support multimodal material understanding.

The work combines dataset development, tactile representation learning, and label quality analysis to study how well LLM-generated annotations align with human judgment.

- Extended the dataset with material-level labels.
- Built a tactile-only encoder-based classifier for material classification.
- Investigated agreement between LLM-annotated and human-labeled categories.
- Studied whether tactile signals alone can classify materials that are visually ambiguous to humans.

This project supports my research on embodied and multimodal AI systems that reason about the physical world beyond appearance alone.
