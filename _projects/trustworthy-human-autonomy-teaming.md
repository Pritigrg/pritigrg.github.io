This project investigates neuro-symbolic reinforcement learning for trustworthy human-autonomy teaming.

The work connects learning-based control with structured reasoning to support safer and more interpretable AI behavior in collaborative settings.

- Explored neuro-symbolic reinforcement learning for trustworthy autonomy.
- Framed the work around assurance and security for AI-enabled systems.
- Presented the research as an oral presentation at SPIE in 2024.

This line of work aligns with my interest in building AI systems that are not only capable, but also dependable in real-world decision contexts.
