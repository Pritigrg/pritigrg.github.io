**Feature Refinement for Person Re-Identification**
<br>Gurung, P., Li, J., and Rawat, D. B.
<br>*Under submission*, 2025
<br>A plug-and-play, training-free method that refines person re-identification embeddings via leave-one-out mean centering. Proposes identity-aware and cluster-aware variants for supervised and label-free settings, improving feature separability and Rank-1 accuracy without retraining.
<br>\[[Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=SHeulucAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao)\]

**Getting the Right Attention: Body-Part Aware Prompting for Person Re-Identification**
<br>Gurung, P., Li, J., and Rawat, D. B.
<br>*AWS Machine Learning University AI/ML Student Poster Session*, 2025 \| Accepted poster
<br>Proposes body-part-aware prompting to guide vision-language models toward semantically meaningful regions, improving attention localization and identity-matching robustness in person re-identification.
<br>\[[Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=SHeulucAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao)\]

**Extended TVL Dataset for Material Classification**
<br>Gurung, P., Li, J., and Rawat, D. B.
<br>*Ongoing*, 2025
<br>Extends the Touch-Vision-Language dataset with material-type annotations. Builds a tactile-only encoder-based classifier and investigates agreement between LLM-generated and human-provided material labels.
<br>\[[Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=SHeulucAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao)\]

**Risk Profiling using LLM and Few-shot Prompting Technique**
<br>Priti Gurung
<br>*NeurIPS Women in Machine Learning Workshop*, 2024 \| Accepted poster
<br>Proposes an automated risk assessment system using LLMs and few-shot prompting with a 0–10 scoring framework spanning financial stability, cybersecurity, and reputation management at social-media scale.
<br>\[[Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=SHeulucAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao)\]
