I am a third-year Computer Science Ph.D. student at Howard University, advised by Dr. [Jiang Li](https://profiles.howard.edu/jiang-li) and Dr. [Danda B. Rawat](https://www.rawat.info/). My research sits at the intersection of computer vision, multimodal AI, and physical world understanding.
